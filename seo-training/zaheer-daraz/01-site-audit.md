# Site audit: Daraz Pakistan

Trainee: Zaheer
Site: https://www.daraz.pk/
Read on: 3 November 2026 (Asia/Karachi)

Five pages were chosen because they match the two blog topics: choosing a smartphone, and paying, receiving and returning an order. Lengths below are character counts of the live title and meta description after decoding HTML entities.

The training rule used on every row: a title stays within 60 characters, puts the focus keyword near the start, and ends with the brand. A description stays between 140 and 155 characters, includes the keyword, and asks the reader to do one clear thing.

## 1. Homepage

- URL: https://www.daraz.pk/
- Live title (72): Online Shopping in Pakistan: Fashion, Electronics & Groceries - Daraz.pk
- Live description (8): Pakistan

The keyword sits at the start and the brand sits at the end, which is the right shape. The title is 12 characters over the limit, so the end is what gets cut in a results page. The description is the word "Pakistan". It has no offer, no keyword phrase, and no next step. A shopper who only reads the snippet learns nothing.

## 2. Smartphones category

- URL: https://www.daraz.pk/smartphones/
- Live title (60): Mobile Price in Pakistan - Buy Smartphones Online - Daraz.pk
- Live description (192): Check Out the Best Smartphone at the Best Price Online in Pakistan at Daraz PK. We Deliver High Quality Mobile Phones in Karachi, Islamabad, Lahore, and All Over the Country. Cash On Delivery.

The title is exactly 60 characters, and it does end with the brand. It leads with "Mobile Price", so the page sounds like a price list. This submission does not invent prices, and the title should not promise a "best" price either. The description is 37 characters over 155. It repeats a quality claim and a city list that do not help someone choose. "Cash On Delivery" is useful, but it is buried at the end.

## 3. Returns and refunds FAQ

- URL: https://www.daraz.pk/returns-refunds-faq/
- Live title (60): Daraz.pk Returns & Refunds FAQs - Your Quick Reference Guide
- Live description (140): Quickly find answers to your questions about Daraz returns & refunds. Our FAQs are your go-to resource for understanding the return process.

Length is inside the rule: the title is 60 and the description is 140. The brand is at the start, not the end, so the keyword "returns" is not the first idea. The description names the topic and points at the FAQ, which is a real next step. It can still be tighter and put the keyword first.

The page itself is the source for a few shopper facts used later in the blogs, in original sentences: a return request within 14 days of delivery, original undamaged manufacturer packaging, no charge to ship the product back, and the option to return only some items in an order. Those facts are not copied here as a table.

## 4. How to return a product

- URL: https://www.daraz.pk/wow/i/pk/help/how-to-return
- Live title (47): Quick and Easy Daraz Products Returns Explained
- Live description (150): Discover how to return products on Daraz with ease. Our comprehensive guide simplifies the Daraz return process for a stress-free shopping experience.

The description length is fine. The title has no brand at the end, and "quick and easy" spends the front of the title on a mood instead of the task. Someone searching for how to return a product should see that task first.

## 5. Buyer Help Center

- URL: https://buyer-helpcenter.daraz.pk/s/page?language=en&pageId=2
- Live title (11): Help Center
- Live description (11): Help Center

This is the weakest page. The title and the description are the same two words. There is no focus keyword, no brand, and no next step. The page itself offers track, return, cancel, payment options, wallet and care contact, but the snippet hides all of that.

## What the five pages share

Support pages are closer to the length rule than the homepage and the smartphones category. The homepage description is thin. The smartphones description is long and full of claims. The Help Center snippet could belong to any company. New titles below put the task or the product first and "Daraz" last. New descriptions stay inside 140–155 characters, name the keyword, and tell the reader what to open next. None of them states a price, a specification, or a "best" claim.
