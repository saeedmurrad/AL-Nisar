# Outline: Online Shopping in Pakistan

Status: outline, before the draft.

## SEO plan

- Focus keyword: online shopping in Pakistan
- Secondary keywords: cash on delivery, Daraz Wallet, return window, help with orders
- H1 is the assigned topic, used once.
- Focus keyword in the first sentence, in one H2, in the meta title, and in the slug.
- Payment and return facts are only the ones published on Daraz’s own returns FAQ and refund policy, and they are paraphrased. The reader is sent to those pages because the tables can change. Daraz Global is called out as outside the refund table, because the policy says so.
