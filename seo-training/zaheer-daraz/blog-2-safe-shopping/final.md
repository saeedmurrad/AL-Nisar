# Online Shopping in Pakistan: Safe Payment, Delivery and Return Tips

Online shopping in Pakistan is safer when you treat the order as a short process, not as a single tap. You open the real site, you pay on its checkout, you look at the parcel before you sign, and you already know how a return works. None of those habits depends on a lucky seller.

This piece does not list prices, and it does not crown a payment method. Daraz publishes the methods and the refund rules. Those pages can change. The habits below send you to the live page before you pay, and again if something arrives that you did not order.

## Shop from the site you opened yourself

Type the address or use a bookmark you saved. A link in a message or a forwarded image can look official and still be someone else’s page. Once you are on [Daraz](https://www.daraz.pk/), stay in that tab until you have paid or decided not to. If a pop-up tells you to “confirm” on a different site, close it.

Use your own account. A shared login hides which address, phone number and wallet the order used. Check the delivery address and the courier’s phone number before you pay. A wrong digit is a simple mistake and a costly one.

## Pay only on the Daraz checkout

The safe payment is the one the checkout offers while you are logged in. Do not transfer money to a personal account because a message said it would hold the item.

Daraz’s refund policy names several ways an order may have been paid: debit or credit card, HBL direct transfer, Daraz Wallet, Easypaisa, cash on delivery, a voucher, JazzCash, and equated monthly instalments. Pay with a method you saw on the checkout. A refund does not always return by the same route. The policy also says its refund table does not apply to Daraz Global products. If a listing badge is unclear, ask before you pay. Read the current table on the [refund policy](https://www.daraz.pk/refund-policy/).

### Cash on delivery

Cash on delivery means you pay when the parcel arrives. People choose it when they want to see that a parcel turned up before money leaves their hand. It is not a check on the item inside. The refund policy says cash-on-delivery refunds go to Daraz Wallet, and that the wallet has to be activated before you can use the amount. If you might return the order, activate the wallet before delivery day.

### Cards, wallets and bank transfer

A card payment stays on the card trail, and a wallet payment stays on the wallet trail, except where the policy says otherwise. It describes some JazzCash and cash-on-delivery refunds as going to Daraz Wallet rather than back to the original app. “I paid with this, so the money will reappear there” is not always true. Open the policy before you buy something you are unsure about.

Do not send a photo of your card, a one-time password, or a screenshot of your balance. Checkout does not need those pictures.

## Delivery day habits

Track the parcel from your account. The [Help Center](https://buyer-helpcenter.daraz.pk/s/page?language=en&pageId=2) puts tracking next to returns, cancellations and payment options. If a courier’s call and the account status disagree, trust the account until you have seen the box.

Look at the outer packing before you sign or pay. If it is crushed, open or wet, say so and photograph it. Then compare the item with the title: the name, the colour if colour mattered, and the pieces the listing promised. For a phone, write those pieces down before you pay, which is the point of the [smartphone buyer’s guide](/how-to-choose-the-right-smartphone-in-pakistan). Keep the packaging. A tidy room is not worth a refused return.

## Use the return window while it is open

Daraz’s [returns and refunds FAQ](https://www.daraz.pk/returns-refunds-faq/) says you can start a return request within 14 days of delivery. The product should go back in the original, undamaged manufacturer packaging. Do not put tape on the manufacturer’s box. The FAQ says there is no charge for shipping the product back, that the shipping you were charged is included in the refund, and that you may return some items without returning the whole order.

Those rules have edges. The FAQ points to items that are not returnable. After 14 days it says a return is not possible, and it points a later fault toward the brand warranty if there is one. Warranty is not the same as a Daraz return.

The [how to return](https://www.daraz.pk/wow/i/pk/help/how-to-return) page is the form: My Orders, the request, then pickup or drop-off. Write the order number and the return tracking number on the outer package, because the page asks you to. Count the 14 days from the delivery date in your account, not from the day you finally opened the box.

## When the order goes wrong

Use help with orders before you send a second payment “to unlock the first one”. Stay in the Help Center. Keep screenshots of the listing, the checkout total and the box, and write down the delivery date.

If a refund seems late, read the policy row for your payment method. The policy says its clock starts once a quality check is finished, and the times differ for a card, a bank transfer, a wallet and cash on delivery. This article will not invent one number for every method. If the item was Daraz Global, do not use the local table. Ask the Help Center which rule applies.

## Questions shoppers ask

### Is cash on delivery safer than paying now?

Only in one way: you still have the cash until a parcel arrives. It does not inspect the item, and a refund is described as landing in Daraz Wallet rather than as cash in your hand. Choose it when that trade is acceptable.

### The box is damaged. Should I refuse it?

Photograph it, tell the courier, and keep the packaging. Then start a return from your account if you do not want the item, inside the published window.

### Can I return one thing from a multi-item order?

The returns FAQ says you do not have to return the whole order. Confirm that your item is returnable first. Do not assume a sale item follows the same rule.

### Where does the money go back to?

It depends how you paid. The refund policy lists the route and says the wallet must be activated for wallet refunds. Read your row. Do not copy a stranger’s comment under a product.
