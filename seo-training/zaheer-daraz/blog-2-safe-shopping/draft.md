# Online Shopping in Pakistan: Safe Payment, Delivery and Return Tips

Status: draft. Not the submitted version.

A parcel is not safe because the listing had a green badge. It is safer when you can explain, in order, which site you used, how you paid, what you checked at the door, and how you would send the item back. That is the whole of careful online shopping in Pakistan. The rest is detail you confirm on Daraz’s own pages, because those pages can change and this draft should not pretend to be the policy.

No prices appear below. No payment method is called the winner.

## Open the real site, on your own account

Type daraz.pk or use a bookmark. A link in a chat can wear the right colours and still be the wrong place. Stay inside the tab you opened. If something tells you to confirm the order on another site, close it.

Shop on an account you control. Shared logins muddle the address, the phone number and the wallet. Before you pay, read the address and the number the courier will dial. One wrong digit is enough to lose an afternoon.

## Pay where the checkout tells you to pay

Anything that asks you to transfer money to a person “so they can hold the item” is not checkout. Daraz’s refund policy names card payments, HBL direct transfer, Daraz Wallet, Easypaisa, cash on delivery, vouchers, JazzCash and instalments. You do not need the list by heart. You need the method that was on the screen, and you need to know a refund may not travel back along the same path. The policy also says the refund table does not cover Daraz Global products. Read the live page at https://www.daraz.pk/refund-policy/ before you rely on a row.

Cash on delivery lets you keep the money until a parcel arrives. It does not inspect what is inside. The policy describes those refunds as going to Daraz Wallet, which has to be activated or you cannot use the amount. Switch the wallet on before delivery day if a return is even slightly likely.

Card and wallet payments mostly stay on their own trail, except where the policy says a JazzCash or cash-on-delivery case is refunded to the wallet instead. Assume nothing about the route until you have read your row. Never send a photo of a card, a one-time password, or a picture of your balance. Checkout does not ask for those.

## At the door

Follow the parcel in your account. A texted code from a stranger is not your order page. If the courier’s story and the account disagree, believe the account until the box is in your hands.

Look at the outer packing before you sign or hand over cash. Crushed, torn or wet packing should be photographed, and you should say so. Then match the contents to the title: name, colour where it mattered, and the pieces the listing promised. If the order is a phone, that match is the whole point of deciding the contents before you paid. Keep every box. A return without packaging is a weaker return.

## The window is a date, not a mood

The returns FAQ at https://www.daraz.pk/returns-refunds-faq/ says you can request a return within 14 days of delivery. Send the item back in the original undamaged manufacturer box, with no tape on that box. The FAQ says you are not charged to ship it back, that shipping you already paid is part of the refund, and that a partial return is allowed. It also points at items that cannot be returned. After the 14 days, the FAQ says the return route is closed and a later fault is a warranty question for the brand, if a warranty exists.

Count from the delivery date in the account. Politeness is a good way to miss the date. The form lives under My Orders on the how-to-return page, https://www.daraz.pk/wow/i/pk/help/how-to-return . Put the order number and the return tracking number on the outside, as that page asks.

## If it goes wrong, do not pay a second time

A message that says a second transfer will unlock the first payment is a reason to stop, not a reason to open your banking app. Use the account’s help tools, save screenshots of the listing, the total and the box, and note the day the parcel arrived. If the refund feels slow, read the row for your method. The policy ties its timing to a quality check, and the times are not identical for cards, bank transfers, wallets and cash on delivery. One number cannot cover all of them, so this draft does not invent one. Global items follow a different note. Ask, using the order you already have.

## Keep a small record

Before the courier arrives, screenshot the order page: the title, the total, the address and the promised contents. You will not remember the exact title once the box is open and the room is busy. Store the picture with the date. If you pay cash on delivery, note that you did, because the refund route described for that method is the wallet, and you will want to know which row to open later.

After delivery, add one more picture of the sealed parcel and one of the contents next to the packing. This is not a legal essay. It is a way to answer the form without reconstructing the afternoon. The final version points this habit at the Help Center, where tracking and returns already sit together. This draft only says: do not trust your memory more than the account.

## Questions this draft still answers plainly

### Is paying at the door the careful choice?

It is careful only about when the cash leaves you. It is not careful about the contents, and the refund path described for it is a wallet, not notes in your hand.

### The carton arrived crushed.

Photograph it, tell the courier, keep it, and start a return inside the window if you do not want the item.

### One item in the order is wrong. Must everything go back?

The FAQ says no, you can return the item that is eligible. Check the non-returnable list before you assume a sale item qualifies.

### Who can tell me the refund has landed?

The policy row for the way you paid, not a comment under the product. Activate the wallet if that row points there.
