# Site audit: Kaya Skin Clinic

Trainee: Amna
Site assigned: https://www.kayaskinclinic.com/
Read on: 3 November 2026 (Asia/Karachi)

The assigned address redirects to the UAE English site, `https://www.kayaskinclinic.com/uae/en`. A `/pk/en/` path returns 404. The five pages below are the live UAE pages closest to the two blog topics and to a first visit. Treatment pages are built in the browser, so the title and description are what a results snippet can rely on. Visible body copy was thin in the first HTML response. That is noted, and it is not filled in with invented clinic statistics.

The training rule: title within 60 characters, focus keyword near the start, brand at the end. Description between 140 and 155 characters, with the keyword and a call to book or read. New copy does not promise a result and does not repeat customer counts.

## 1. Homepage

- URL: https://www.kayaskinclinic.com/uae/en
- Live title (68): Kaya Skin Clinic UAE – Skin Care, Body And Hair Treatment Specialist
- Live description (158): Kaya Skin Clinic is one of the largest skincare clinics in Middle East with 20 clinics in the region, 24 dermatologists, and over 350,000 satisfied customers.

The title is 8 characters over 60, and it starts with the brand instead of the service. The description is 3 characters over 155. It spends that space on size and customer figures. The homepage text retrieved with the page showed different figures ("15 UAE Clinics" and "600,000 Customers"). The snippet and the page do not match. New copy leaves the numbers out. A conflicting figure is worse than no figure.

The visible heading on the page was "Beauty that runs deep". That line does not say what the clinic treats, so the meta description has to do that job.

## 2. Laser hair removal

- URL: https://www.kayaskinclinic.com/uae/en/treatments/laser-hair-removal
- Live title (82): Laser Hair Removal in UAE | Long-Term Hair Reduction and Smooth Skin | Kaya Clinic
- Live description (187): Explore Laser Hair Removal in UAE at Kaya Clinic for smoother skin, reduced maintenance, and long-term hair reduction tailored to diverse skin tones, treatment areas, and lifestyle needs.

The keyword is in the right place, and the brand is at the end. Both fields are far too long: 22 characters over on the title and 32 on the description. The extra words are result language ("long-term hair reduction", "smoother skin", "reduced maintenance"). The training rule for clinic writing is no promise of results. A snippet should offer a consultation, not an outcome.

## 3. Acne and acne scars

- URL: https://www.kayaskinclinic.com/uae/en/treatments/acne-acne-scar
- Live title (79): Acne Treatment in UAE | Pimples, Oily Skin and Breakout Solutions | Kaya Clinic
- Live description (213): Ice pick, boxcar, rolling scars, or acne marks? Learn the different types of acne scars, compare laser vs chemical peel treatments, and discover how many sessions you may need. Expert guide from Kaya Clinic Dubai.

The title is 19 characters over, and "solutions" sounds like a cure. The description is 58 characters over. "How many sessions you may need" teases a number this submission will not invent, and it implies the article can prescribe a course. The brand is present, which is good. The city "Dubai" appears only at the end of a description that is already about the UAE page.

## 4. About the clinic

- URL: https://www.kayaskinclinic.com/uae/en/about-us
- Live title (85): The Kaya Skin Clinic Story - Trusted by over 350,000 Customers | Kaya Skin Clinic UAE
- Live description (166): Kaya Skin Clinic, an international chain specialises in advanced dermatological solutions for women and men, with its commitment to comprehensive aesthetic skin care.

The title is 25 characters over, mostly because it repeats the brand and inserts a customer count that does not match the homepage snippet (350,000 in both of these, against 600,000 on the homepage text). The description is 11 characters over and says "solutions", which is a soft promise. A first-visit page should say who the clinic is for and how to book.

## 5. Meet the experts

- URL: https://www.kayaskinclinic.com/uae/en/meet-the-experts
- Live title (69): Meet Our Best Dermatologist in United Arab Emirates| Kaya Skin Clinic
- Live description (169): Meet the team of best dermatologists at Kaya Skin Clinic UAE. Our team of highly qualified specialists offers personalized skin and hair care. Book an appointment today!

The description does have a call to action, which the other pages often miss. It is 14 characters over, and "best" is a ranking the page does not need. The title is 9 characters over, misses a space before the pipe, and says "dermatologist" in the singular while the page is a team. The keyword is close to the start. That part can stay.

## What the five pages share

Keyword and brand placement is often half right, and length is the failure. Clinic pages also use result language and headcount figures that disagree with each other. The replacements stay inside the character rules, name the service first, end with "Kaya Clinic", and invite a consultation or a read. They do not say a treatment will work, and they do not quote a number of clinics, doctors, customers or sessions.
