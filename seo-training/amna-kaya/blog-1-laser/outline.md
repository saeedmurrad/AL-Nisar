# Outline: Laser Hair Removal

Status: outline, before the draft.

## SEO plan

- Focus keyword: laser hair removal
- Secondary keywords: before laser hair removal, during a laser visit, after a laser visit, hair removal consultation
- H1 is the assigned topic, used once.
- The focus keyword is in the first sentence, in one H2, in the meta title, and in the slug.
